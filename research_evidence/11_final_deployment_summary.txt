============================================================
OVERALL DEPLOYMENT FINDING
============================================================

The deployment experiments demonstrate the technical feasibility of
running the MobileNetV3-based TB Guard screening prototype on a
Raspberry Pi 400 with local inference, Grad-CAM explanation support,
low runtime memory requirements, automatic application startup,
database backup/recovery, and offline software recovery capability.

The prototype is intended for tuberculosis screening research and
decision-support evaluation and is not presented as a replacement for
clinical diagnosis.
